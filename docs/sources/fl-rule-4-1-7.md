# Rule 4-1.7, Rules Regulating The Florida Bar

Text as pasted by Josh on 2026-11-03 (amended May 29, 2014, effective June 1, 2014, 140 So.3d 541). Key passages relied on:

- (b)(1)–(4): reasonable belief; not prohibited by law; not same proceeding before a tribunal; informed consent of each affected client, confirmed in writing.
- (c): common representation in a single matter requires explaining its implications, advantages and risks.
- Comment, Other conflict situations: "a lawyer may not represent multiple parties to a negotiation whose interests are fundamentally antagonistic to each other, but common representation is permissible where the clients are generally aligned in interest even though there is some difference of interest among them."
- Comment, Consultation and consent: "when a disinterested lawyer would conclude that the client should not agree to the representation under the circumstances, the lawyer involved cannot properly ask for such agreement."
