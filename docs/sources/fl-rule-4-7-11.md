# Rule 4-7.11, Rules Regulating The Florida Bar

Text as pasted by Josh on 2026-11-03 (adopted January 31, 2013, effective May 1, 2013, 108 So.3d 609). Key passages relied on:

- (a): applies "to all forms of communication in any print or electronic forum, including ... Internet, including banners, pop-ups, websites, social networking, and video sharing media. The terms 'advertising' and 'advertisement' as used in chapter 4-7 refer to all forms of communication seeking legal employment, both written and spoken."
- (b): applies to lawyers who advertise legal services in Florida or target Florida residents.
- (c): "This subchapter applies to communications made to referral sources about legal services."
- Comment: websites are treated the same as other advertising media; advertisements disseminated only in another jurisdiction where the Florida Bar member is admitted, complying with that jurisdiction's rules and not targeted at Florida residents, are outside subchapter 4-7.
