# Rule 4-5.7, Rules Regulating The Florida Bar

Text as pasted by Josh on 2026-11-03 (added effective April 25, 2002, 820 So.2d 210). Key passages relied on:

- (a): a lawyer providing nonlegal services "not distinct from legal services provided to that recipient is subject to the Rules Regulating The Florida Bar with respect to the provision of both legal and nonlegal services."
- (b), (c): for distinct services, or services through an affiliated entity, the Rules apply "if the lawyer knows or reasonably should know that the recipient might believe that the recipient is receiving the protection of a client-lawyer relationship."
- (d): (b) and (c) do not apply if the lawyer makes reasonable efforts to avoid misunderstanding, including "advising the recipient, preferably in writing, that the services are not legal services and that the protection of a client-lawyer relationship does not exist."
- Comment: when services are entwined, the lawyer "must take special care to heed the proscriptions ... addressing conflict of interest and to scrupulously adhere to the requirements of the rule relating to disclosure of confidential information. The promotion of the nonlegal services must also in all respects comply with the Rules ... dealing with advertising and solicitation." The communication "should be made before entering into an agreement ... and preferably should be in writing." "The burden is upon the lawyer to show that the lawyer has taken reasonable measures."
