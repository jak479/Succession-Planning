# Rule 4-5.4, Rules Regulating The Florida Bar

Text as pasted by Josh on 2026-11-03 (amended through June 2, 2022, effective August 1, 2022, SC22-607). Key passages relied on:

- (a): "A lawyer or law firm shall not share legal fees with a nonlawyer," except (1)–(5) (death-benefit agreements, a deceased lawyer's unfinished business, purchase of a practice under 4-1.17, nonlawyer employee bonuses not based on clients brought in or calculated as a percentage of fees, court-awarded fees with a pro bono organization).
- (c): "A lawyer shall not form a partnership with a nonlawyer if any of the activities of the partnership consist of the practice of law."
- (d): "A lawyer shall not permit a person who recommends, employs, or pays the lawyer to render legal services for another to direct or regulate the lawyer's professional judgment in rendering such legal services."
- (e): no practice in a for-profit entity in which a nonlawyer owns an interest, is a director or officer, or may direct the lawyer's judgment.
- Comment: "Where someone other than the client pays the lawyer's fee or salary, or recommends employment of the lawyer, that arrangement does not modify the lawyer's obligation to the client"; see rule 4-1.8(f). "Compensation plans, as opposed to retirement plans, may not be based on legal fees."
