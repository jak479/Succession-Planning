# Rule 4-7.16, Rules Regulating The Florida Bar

Text as pasted by Josh on 2026-11-03. Key passages relied on:

- Lead-in: listed information "is presumed not to violate the provisions of rules 4-7.11 through 4-7.15."
- (a)(1)–(12): name, lawyers, offices, contact details, "attorney" or "law firm"; dates of admission to The Florida Bar and other bars, years of experience, other jurisdictions; licenses and degrees; military service; languages; "fields of law in which the lawyer practices ... subject to the requirements of this subchapter regarding use of terms such as certified, specialist, and expert"; prepaid plans; credit cards; "fee for initial consultation and fee schedule, subject to the requirements of this subchapter regarding cost disclosures and honoring advertised fees"; salutary language; listed illustrations, including a photograph of the lawyer against a plain background.
- Comment: "A lawyer desiring a safe harbor from discipline may choose to limit the content of an advertisement to the information listed in this rule and, if the information is true, the advertisement complies with these rules. However, a lawyer is not required to limit the information ... as long as all information in the advertisement complies with these rules."
