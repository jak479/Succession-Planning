# Rule 4-7.19, Rules Regulating The Florida Bar

Text as pasted by Josh on 2026-11-03 (amended May 15, 2025, effective July 14, 2025, SC2025-0018). Key passages relied on:

- (a): file "a copy of each advertisement at least 20 days prior to the lawyer's first dissemination," subject to 4-7.20 exemptions, in the manner posted on the Bar's website.
- (b): if the Bar sends no communication within 15 days of a complete filing, "the lawyer will not be subject to discipline," except for dissemination after a later notice of noncompliance.
- (d): opinions on exempt advertisements may be requested; "a lawyer may not file an entire website for review," but may submit "a specific page, provision, statement, illustration, or photograph."
- (e): evaluation is for facial compliance only; it "does not relieve the lawyer of responsibility for the accuracy of factual statements."
- (f): a finding of compliance binds the Bar in a grievance "unless the advertisement contains a misrepresentation that is not apparent from the face of the advertisement."
- (h): contents of a filing, including all media, frequency and period of use, the responsible lawyer, and a fee set by the executive director.
- (i): refile after a change of circumstances that raises a substantial possibility the ad has become false or misleading; additional fee not to exceed $100.
- (j): retain a copy "for 3 years after its last dissemination along with a record of when and where it was used."
- Comment: an exempt advertisement may be submitted for a safe harbor; a notice of compliance makes the lawyer "immune from grievance liability," with the same exception.
