# Rule 4-7.14, Rules Regulating The Florida Bar

Text as pasted by Josh on 2026-11-03 (amended through June 22, 2023, effective August 21, 2023, SC22-1294). Key passages relied on:

- (a)(1): advertisements "subject to varying reasonable interpretations, 1 or more of which would be materially misleading"; (a)(2): "literally accurate, but could reasonably mislead a prospective client regarding a material fact."
- (a)(5)–(6): no "specialist" or "expert" claim unless certified under the Florida Certification Plan or an accredited plan, or objectively verifiable "based on the lawyer's education, training, experience, or substantial involvement."
- (a)(7): fee information must disclose "all fees and expenses for which the client might be liable and any other material information relating to the fee"; an advertised fee or range must be honored "for at least 90 days unless the advertisement specifies a shorter period."
- (b): clarifying information may cure a potentially misleading advertisement.
- Comment: a lawyer "may indicate that the lawyer concentrates in, focuses on, or limits the lawyer's practice to particular areas of practice as long as the statements are true."
